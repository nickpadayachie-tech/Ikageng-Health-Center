Maternal and women's health

What this service is

Family planning means choosing whether, when and how to have children — and having the information and support to make those decisions well. It also includes advice on contraception, fertility awareness, sexual health and how family planning fits into your overall health.

Every person’s situation is different. The number of children you have, your health, your work, your partner’s wishes and what you can afford all matter. We will talk through the options without judgement and help you decide what is realistic for you.

No method suits everyone, and side effects and effectiveness vary from one method to another. We explain the options in plain language, and you can change your mind later. Your choice is yours.

Good to know

Confidentiality
Your consultation is private. Nothing is shared with family or partners without your consent.
Bring with you
The name of any method you currently use, and a list of any medication you take.
Also covered
HIV testing, sexual health counselling and cervical screening advice can all be discussed.

Family planning should always be a decision you make freely, without pressure. If you ever feel that is not the case, please tell us.

At your visit

What to expect

Every visit is a little different, but these are the steps you can expect for this service at Ikageng Health Centre.

A private conversation

We talk about your reproductive health goals, your history and what is important to you at this stage.

Health review

Your general health, blood pressure and relevant medical history are checked before anything is recommended.

Your options explained

Each available method is described in plain language, including how it works, how effective it is and what to expect.

Supply or prescription

The method you choose is provided or prescribed, with clear instructions for use.

Side effects and advice

We cover what is common, what is not, and exactly what to do if something does not suit you.

Follow-up

A review date is set so that we can check how you are getting on and adjust if needed.

Reasons to visit

Why people come to us

If any of these sound like you, please come in — or phone us first if you would like to talk it through before you do.

  • You want to start a contraceptive method, or change the one you are using.
  • You would like advice on spacing pregnancies.
  • You have stopped using a method and need cover.
  • You have questions or concerns about side effects, or about fertility and planning a pregnancy.

Before you come

  • Bring your clinic card or any records you have.
  • Bring a list of any medication and supplements you take.
  • Bring the results of any tests done elsewhere.
  • Write down your questions beforehand — it helps.
  • Bring someone with you if you would like support.

All of our conversations are private. You are welcome to ask questions at any point, and you can ask to speak alone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, phone us on 072 715 6115 — we are happy to talk it through.

Is my consultation confidential?

Yes. What you discuss is between you and the practitioner. We will involve anyone else only with your permission.

Will you pressure me into a decision?

No. We will explain your options and the practical considerations, and the decision is entirely yours to make and to change.

Can I change methods later?

Yes, and plenty of people do. Let us know if something is not working for you — it is far easier to adjust than to put up with a method you are unhappy with.

Do you have contraceptive stock available?

Stock changes from week to week. Please phone 072 715 6115 before you come in and we will tell you what is available on the day.
