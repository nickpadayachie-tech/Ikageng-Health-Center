Welcome

A Health Centre built around the People of Ikageng

Ikageng Health Centre is a healthcare clinic at 1539 Lekhele Street, Ikageng Location, Potchefstroom. We focus on the everyday needs of our community — from the first weeks of pregnancy through to childhood, preventive screening and the ongoing management of long-term conditions.

Our approach is straightforward. We listen, we explain what we find in plain language, and we help you decide what to do next. Every consultation is an opportunity to ask questions, and we would rather you leave understanding your health than simply having been seen.

Whether you are here for a routine check, for advice on a long-term condition, or because something is worrying you, you will find a calm space, clear information and a practitioner who takes the time to listen.

Health education

Why preventive healthcare matters

Many health conditions are easier to manage when they are found early. Preventive care is not about reacting to illness — it is about noticing changes in good time, and making informed decisions before a problem becomes a crisis.

Screening catches changes early

Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and haemoglobin checks can identify risks while they are still easy to act on. A single measurement is a snapshot; regular checks give a far clearer picture of your health over time.

Immunisation protects the whole community

Vaccination works best when most of a community is protected. Keeping your own immunisation up to date is one of the simplest ways to reduce the risk of serious illness for yourself, your family and those around you.

Regular maternal healthcare

Antenatal visits and postnatal follow-up give you and your baby consistent support, and make it easier to raise concerns early — physical, emotional or practical.

Ongoing monitoring of chronic conditions

If you live with a long-term condition such as diabetes or hypertension, regular reviews help keep your treatment aligned with how you actually feel, and reduce the risk of complications.

Our approach

Patient Centred, from the first hello

Good healthcare starts with trust. That means taking the time to understand what is really troubling you, explaining your options honestly, and making sure you leave knowing what happens next.

We are a small, local team serving Ikageng and the wider Potchefstroom area. We know that reaching a clinic is not always easy — cost, transport, time away from work or family, and simply not knowing where to start all matter. If you are unsure whether something is worth coming in for, please phone us. We would rather answer a question than have a worry go unaddressed.
