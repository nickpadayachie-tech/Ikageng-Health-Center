Send an enquiry

Write to us

Fill in the form and your email application will open with the enquiry ready to send. Nothing is sent automatically — you press send, and we will pick it up during clinic hours.

We use this only to reply to your enquiry.

Please do not include detailed medical records. If you need to discuss a medical result, please phone us instead.

Prefer to write directly? Email us at sesi.mogoera@ikagenghealthcenter.co.za, or use our alternative address mogoerasesi@gmail.com.

Finding the Health Centre

We are at 1539 Lekhele Street, Ikageng Location, Potchefstroom, North West, South Africa.

The clinic is on the Ikageng Location section of Lekhele Street. If you are not sure of the exact entrance, phone us before you travel and we will talk you through it.

Parking and public transport information changes from time to time, so please call ahead if that is a concern for your visit.
