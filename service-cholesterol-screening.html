Preventive Screening

What this service is

Cholesterol is a fatty substance your body needs, but when the balance of cholesterol and triglycerides in the blood is wrong, it builds up inside the walls of your arteries. That process is one of the main causes of heart disease and stroke.

A cholesterol check is usually done from a blood sample. Because the result is influenced by what you have eaten and how active you have been, we may ask you to fast for the most accurate reading.

Cholesterol is one part of a wider picture. We look at it alongside blood pressure, blood glucose, weight and your family history, because your overall risk matters more than any single number.

Good to know

Fasting
A fasting test gives the most accurate result. We will confirm the fasting instructions when you book.
Results
Laboratory results usually take a few days. We will tell you when to expect them and how you will hear.
Also covered
Blood pressure and blood glucose are often checked alongside cholesterol.

Cholesterol is one number in a wider assessment. If your result is raised, we will talk it through rather than simply handing you a figure.

At your visit

What to expect

Every visit is a little different, but these are the steps you can expect for this service at Ikageng Health Centre.

Blood sample

A sample is taken for total cholesterol, HDL, LDL and triglycerides.

Habits review

A short conversation about diet, activity, smoking and alcohol, and anything that has changed recently.

Comparison

Your result is compared with previous results if we hold them, so that the trend is visible.

Explanation

We explain what your numbers mean and, just as importantly, what they do not mean on their own.

Practical steps

Advice on food, movement and other habits that genuinely lower risk, including small changes that are realistic.

Reasons to visit

Why people come to us

If any of these sound like you, please come in — or phone us first if you would like to talk it through before you do.

  • You have never had your cholesterol checked.
  • You have a family history of heart disease or stroke.
  • You are over 35, or over 45 if you smoke.
  • You live with diabetes, hypertension or raised blood glucose.
  • You want to reduce your risk but do not know where to start.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, phone us on 067 575 2528 — we are happy to talk it through.

Does eating affect the result?

Yes. Food in the hours before a test can raise triglycerides and lower the measured HDL, so a fasting test gives the clearest picture.

How long do I have to fast?

Usually nothing to eat or drink other than water for around 9 to 12 hours. Please confirm the instructions when you book your appointment.

Is a high result bad?

It is a risk factor that can be changed. One result does not define your risk — it is one part of a wider assessment.

Can I lower my cholesterol without medication?

Diet, weight, activity and stopping smoking all have a real effect. Some people also need medication, and we will talk that through if it applies to you.
