Every service we offer

Choose a service to read what it involves, what to expect at your visit, and the questions we are most often asked.

Maternal and women's health

Support before, during and after pregnancy, and the reproductive health care that surrounds it.

Antenatal Care

Regular monitoring, health guidance and a listening ear through every stage of your pregnancy.

About antenatal care

Postnatal Care

Follow-up support after birth for the mother, the baby, and the family adjusting to a new routine.

About postnatal care

Family Planning

Non-judgemental conversations about contraception, reproductive health and the choices that suit your life.

About family planning

Clinical programmes in this category. Our antenatal care is delivered using BANC Plus (Basic Antenatal Care Plus), and PMTCT care (Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV) forms part of both antenatal care and HIV testing and counselling. See our practitioner’s training and registration.

Preventive screening

Checks that find problems early, when they are easiest to treat and least disruptive to your life.

HIV Testing and Counselling

Confidential HIV testing with counselling before and after, in a private and non-judgemental space.

About HIV testing and counselling

Diabetic Screening

Blood glucose checks to spot diabetes early — and regular monitoring if you already have it.

About diabetic screening

Hypertension Screening

Blood pressure checks — a few minutes that can catch a condition with no symptoms at all.

About hypertension screening

Cholesterol Screening

Cholesterol and lipid checks — a clearer picture of your heart and blood vessel health.

About cholesterol screening

Haemoglobin (Hb) Screening

A quick blood test for anaemia — the low iron that so often goes unnoticed.

About Hb screening

Pap Smear

Routine cervical screening — a short, simple check that helps prevent cervical cancer.

About Pap smears

Clinical programme: PMTCT. PMTCT (Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV) is part of our care when pregnancy or planning for pregnancy is a factor. See our practitioner’s training and registration.

Child health

Immunisation and illness assessment for the children in our community.

Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)

Systematic assessment and treatment for common illnesses in children under five.

About IMCI

Immunisation

Vaccines for you and your children, on schedule, protecting the whole community.

About immunisation

Ongoing care

Keeping long-term conditions under control, and treating the everyday complaints that come up in between.

Chronic Condition Care and Monitoring

Regular review and support for long-term conditions, so that your treatment keeps up with your life.

About chronic condition care

Sister on Call after hours

After-hours telephone advice and guidance from our nursing sister when the clinic is closed.

About after-hours sister
